## Description

PolicyPilot is a browser-local decision-support tool for school learning-support teams. It compares synthetic learner profiles under the same policy before the policy reaches a real learner.

In this walkthrough:
- baseline 14-day pressure trajectory;
- a counterfactual where gaming is removed;
- the same policy applied to an action-oriented learner;
- the transparent model receipt and equation;
- the school-team customer, paid pilot model, and scalability path.

Live demo: https://xqscora.github.io/policypilot/?demo=1
Source: https://github.com/xqscora/policypilot

This is an educational simulation, not a mental-health assessment, risk score, diagnosis, or treatment recommender. The demo uses synthetic data and keeps the scenario local. AI coding assistance was used for implementation drafting and debugging; the project owner reviewed the code, assumptions, claims, and limitations.
